Audit work

Klaviyo Lifecycle Audit — Haven

Women's Basics & Loungewear · DTC Apparel. Haven was acquiring well but retaining poorly. I audited the full Klaviyo account and surfaced five prioritized findings.

11.3% 90-day repeat purchase vs 25–30% peer benchmark
58.4% Welcome open rate vs 51.9% peer · strongest flow
3.2% Winback recovery rate weakest flow in account
$1.86 Post-purchase rev/recipient vs $4.90 peer benchmark
Bottom line: Haven is acquiring well but not retaining. The opportunity is in deepening the post-purchase journey, reducing discount dependency, and making every send feel personally relevant to where the customer is in their relationship with the brand.
High priority First-to-second purchase drop-off is the biggest revenue gap ▾

Post-purchase opens are solid at 54.2%, but 90-day repeat purchase sits at 11.3% — well below the 25–30% benchmark. The sequence makes multiple competing asks without a clear progression.

Rebuild post-purchase around a confidence-first arc: reinforce, educate, then one clear next action. Measure by 90-day repeat rate, not email engagement.

High priority Campaign strategy is discount-dependent and margin-eroding ▾

Discount sends outperform non-promo, but the gap is narrowing as customers get conditioned to wait for offers. Broad CTAs consistently underperform product-specific paths.

Build a planned promo-to-editorial ratio. Use behavioral segments to earn the click without discounting.

Medium-high Winback is triggering too late, leading with discount ▾

31.7% open, 1.04% click, 3.2% recovery — the weakest flow in the account. Discount-first winback trains customers to lapse on purpose.

Shorten the trigger window. Lead with brand inspiration. Reserve any discount for the final send only.

Medium-high Segmentation is engagement-based, missing behavioral signals ▾

Audiences built on recency alone — a loungewear buyer and an outerwear browser get the same campaign. Browse behavior, category interest, and purchase history are available but not activated.

Build behavioral and product-interest segments for at least two upcoming sends. Let past purchase inform what's shown next.

Watch Welcome flow is strong, but acquisition mix is creating a ceiling ▾

58.4% open rate is above benchmark, but incentive-led and contest entrants convert worse downstream than direct or browse-intent sign-ups.

Run a source-level welcome analysis. Consider a longer nurture arc before purchase messaging for incentive-led entrants.
